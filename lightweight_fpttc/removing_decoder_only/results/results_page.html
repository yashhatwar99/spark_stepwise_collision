Light FP-TTC Results

1) Removing decoder only (speed up ,accuracy down)

The full FP-TTC next to a light version where only the decoder is replaced. Everything else is the same and frozen: the backbone, the flow + affine stage and the scale encoder, with the released weights.

Only the decoder is removed. The backbone, the flow + affine stage and the scale encoder are exactly the same as in the full model. The decoder is replaced by three plain convolution layers (Conv3). Nothing else was changed.

The light model is smaller and faster, and somewhat less accurate. It has 45% fewer parameters (6.83 M against 12.44 M), runs 15% faster on the GPU (247 ms against 292 ms) and 24% faster on the CPU.

Its Mid error on the 40 held-out KITTI pairs is 68.9 against 54.7 for the full model (+26%, worse). For per-car TTC the two are close: on nuScenes the scale error is the same (0.34% and 0.34%), and the middle TTC error is 1.04 s against 0.94 s.

What changed in the model

The full decoder (the matching-clue attention and two recurrent blocks, 5.97 M parameters) is removed. The light model puts three plain convolution layers (Conv3, 0.37 M parameters) on the scale encoder's output joined with the later frame's image features. Their result is stretched four times with a plain bilinear resize. The model stops right after the scale encoder, so the removed parts do not run.

Size and speed

One KITTI pair at 640×320, the model alone (YOLO and drawing not included), on the laptop's RTX 3050. GPU time is the middle of 30 runs after a warm-up, CPU time the middle of 3 runs.

Peak GPU memory is the same, 0.74 GB for both. The light run still keeps all of the full model's weights loaded, including the unused decoder. The decoder is only part of the time: the backbone's transformer is about half of it, and that is unchanged.

How the decoder was trained

  • Data: KITTI's training pairs. 140 pairs to learn from, 20 to pick the best round, and the 40 held-out pairs kept for the final scores.
  • Loss: the paper's: the gap between the log of the guess and the log of the true scale, over the pixels where KITTI has a true scale.
  • Only Conv3 learns. The rest of the model is frozen. The best round was picked by the 20 quiz pairs, after 66 rounds.
  • First run: a higher learning rate was unstable and gave about twice the error. It was lowered and the final numbers above come from the second run.

Read these numbers with care

  • This is our version, not the authors'. The paper does not say how it turns the Conv3 output back to full size, or exactly what x_t is. We chose a bilinear resize and the later frame's fine image features.
  • Only 160 pairs to learn from, and no pretraining. More data, or learning from the full model's own outputs on many more frames, would probably close some of the gap. That is untested.
  • The 85 held-out KITTI cars are few, so small differences there mean little. nuScenes has 1,418 cars.
  • This is the first step. The flow + affine stage is still in here. Removing it is part 2, below.

2) Removing flow + affine

Two versions, both without the flow + affine stage. The scale encoder keeps its released weights, and Conv3 sits on top as in part 1.

  • A: only flow + affine is removed. The encoder's own scale_conv stays and makes the query from the image features.
  • B: flow + affine and scale_conv are removed. The query is one fixed constant (what scale_conv gives for an all-zero input).
  • As it is: Conv3 from part 1, not trained again. Retrained: Conv3 trained again on KITTI's 140 training pairs with the encoder frozen; the 20 quiz pairs pick the best round.

Why this works: the transformer features are the same with or without the flow stage (checked, difference 0.0000), and the scale encoder's output changes by only 0.51% when its query is made from the image features instead.

Distillation helps by about 2 to 3 points over learning from the truth. More frames, and a truth stage afterwards, add nothing: Conv3 is too small to copy the full model any closer. The 413 raw-drive pairs come from KITTI raw drive 0009; frames near the held-out and quiz scenes were left out (the drive contains KITTI scene flow scenes 7 to 12).
